Chinese Pinyin Function 和 hé And / with 也 yě Also / too 还 hái Additionally / still 或者 huòzhě Or, generally in statements 还是 háishi Or, in alternative questions 和 — Connecting nouns or expressing accompaniment 我要鸡肉和米饭。 I want chicken and rice. 我和朋友吃饭。 I eat with my friends. 也 — Sharing the same action or characteristic 我喜欢中国菜。我朋友也喜欢中国菜。 I like Chinese food. My friend also likes Chinese food. 还 — Adding something 我要鸡肉，还要汤。 I want chicken, and I’d also like soup. 或者 — Alternatives in statements 我喝茶或者咖啡。 I drink tea or coffee. 还是 — Asking someone to choose 你喝茶还是咖啡？ Do you drink tea or coffee?
